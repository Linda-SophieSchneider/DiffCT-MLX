Migrating from 2.x to 3.0

DiffCT-MLX 3.0 changes two defaults and replaces the Torch engine. Code that runs on 2.x still runs on 3.0, but its numbers change. They move by half a detector pixel in position and by the factor voxel_spacing in amplitude.

In 3.0 det_center is the geometric centre of the panel on both backends, the convention of upstream diffct ≥ 2.0. The quantitative FDK path already returned 1/mm in 2.x and does not change.

Reproducing 2.x results

The operator factories and case builders take the old conventions as keywords:

import diffct_mlx as dct

A = dct.make_cone_3d_operator(
    src, det_c, det_u, det_v,
    volume_shape=(D, H, W), detector_shape=(nu, nv),
    du=du, dv=dv, voxel_spacing=vs,
    detector_convention="legacy", line_integrals="voxel",
)

make_parallel_2d_operator, make_fan_2d_operator and the make_*_operators builders accept the same two keywords. The 2.3 name detector_convention="kernel" is an alias of "legacy".

For direct projector calls and for FBP/FDK, convert the detector centre:

det_c_legacy = dct.kernel_detector_center(
    det_c, det_u, du, det_v, dv, convention="legacy")
sino_2x_grid = dct.cone_forward(vol, src, det_c_legacy, det_u, det_v,
                                nu, nv, du, dv, vs)
sino_2x = sino_2x_grid / vs          # voxel units, as 2.x returned

For a parallel beam pass det_origin in place of det_center.

Projection stacks

.npy stacks written by 2.x projectors are in voxel units. Load them with NpyProjectionsConfig(projection_units="voxel") and they are converted to physical units. Measured -log data is physical already and needs no change.

Imports of the engine

The wheel no longer installs a top-level diffct package, so it no longer shadows the PyPI package diffct. Replace

from diffct import ConeProjectorFunction, circular_trajectory_3d

with

from diffct_mlx.engine import ConeProjectorFunction, circular_trajectory_3d

diffct.differentiable is gone. Geometry generators in the engine default to device="cuda" as upstream does. The unified diffct_mlx generators choose the device for you.

Behaviour fixes you will notice

  • Footprint amplitude. The footprint projectors are mass-conserving since 2.2.0, and 3.0 keeps this on the new engine. Coming from 2.1 or the dev0 line, expect a change. Those weighted voxels with 1/M(x)², which biased off-centre voxels by depth, by about 7 % for a typical micro-CT geometry.

  • Metal cone geometry gradient. It now matches the engine’s exact gradient. The 2.x kernel differentiated a trilinear volume and was off by about 100 %. Pose and trajectory optimization on Apple Silicon behaves differently, and correctly.

  • Rays clipped to the source-detector segment. A source or detector inside the volume no longer gives the full-line integral.

Staying on 2.x

Pin diffct-mlx<3 to keep the old behaviour unchanged. 2.3.0 is the last 2.x release.
